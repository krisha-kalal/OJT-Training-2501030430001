Our Story

From urban greenhouse to your living space.

Bred from a deep passion for thoughtful, modern spaces.

Found in 2021, Leafloom was born out of a clear observation: modern urban living spaces are often missing organic warmth. Most industrial outlets treat plants as mere inventory, disregarding their need for proper care and their role in interior aesthetics.

We do things differently. We don't just ship plants; we curate them. Every specimen is selected for its structural integrity, acclimated to home conditions in our greenhouses, and paired with artisanal vessels that enhance your home's unique character.

Why Leafloom?

Curated Quality

We hand-select each plant, ensuring it meets the highest standards of foliage health and structural integrity before it leaves our greenhouse.

Designed for Home

Our plants are meticulously acclimated to indoor light and humidity, ensuring they thrive in your space rather than just surviving.

Sustainable Craft

We collaborate with master ceramicists to create breathable, porous vessels that prioritize root health and timeless minimalist aesthetics.
