Our Shipping Policy

We ensure your botanical companions travel safely from our studio to your living room with maximum care and zero stress.

01. Greenhouse Selection

Each plant is hand-picked by our lead botanist only after it passes a rigorous health and foliage density inspection.

02. Engineered Packaging

We use custom, breathable corrugated containers with reinforced internal bracing to prevent soil shifting or leaf damage.

03. Express Transit

Your order is dispatched via our priority climate-controlled logistics partners, ensuring the plants stay in a stable environment.

04. Doorstep Arrival

Once delivered, your plant comes with a QR code guide to help it acclimate to your home’s specific lighting and humidity.
